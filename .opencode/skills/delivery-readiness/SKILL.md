# Delivery Readiness

- Confirm acceptance criteria, tests, documentation, compatibility, security/privacy checks, and known limitations.
- Distinguish build success from release readiness.
- Require fresh evidence for changed high-risk surfaces.
- Record unresolved issues, rollback/recovery considerations, and Human approval requirements.
- A failed or unknown mandatory gate means not ready.
